# Steering Committee

The charter sets the Committee at five to seven individuals. Each founding member seeds one seat, and no organization holds more than one seat. Seats run 12 months.

No seats are filled. The founding members select the first Committee in [Select the first Steering Committee](https://forum.quantumalliance.foundation/t/20). The selection closes on 2026-12-09.
